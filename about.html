About the workshop

Autokorjaamo Hakola is a small car repair shop in Kauhava. The workshop is run by Pertti Hakola and his son.

We work with cars and also provide repair services for vans and farm vehicles.

Our approach

We want to make it straightforward for customers to tell us what their vehicle needs. An online service request helps us receive the important details before we call the customer.

What we do

Our work includes servicing, brakes, tyres, inspection preparation and general repairs.
